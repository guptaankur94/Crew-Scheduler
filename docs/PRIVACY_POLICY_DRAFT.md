## The short version

- Your roster, plan and settings stay **on your phone** (or in your browser). We have no account system and no
  database of users.
- When **you choose** to read a roster with AI, the image or text is sent through our server to **Google Gemini**
  to be read, and the result comes back to your phone. We do not keep a copy.
- **No ads, no analytics or tracking, no selling of data, and nothing is ever shared with your airline or employer.**
- You can export or delete everything at any time in Settings.

## What is stored on your device

| What | Why | How to remove it |
|---|---|---|
| Your roster as read and confirmed, and your updates (delays, standby changes, callouts, cancellations) | To work out your plan | Rosters → Remove, or Settings → Delete all my data |
| Settings (home base, travel and getting-ready times, usual sleep, caffeine, reminders) | To personalise the plan | Settings → Delete all my data |
| Change history (for Undo and Restore) | So you can undo changes | Settings → Delete all my data |
| The original roster files, if you chose to keep them | To read a roster again without uploading it | Settings → kept files, or Delete all my data |
| Your consent to AI reading, with its date | To ask only once | Settings → withdraw |

We do not store your name. Earlier versions kept the name printed on a roster; it is removed when the app opens.

## What leaves your device, and when

**Only when you upload a roster and have agreed to AI reading:** the image, PDF or pasted text, your home base and
which clock your roster's times are in. It goes over an encrypted connection to our server, which passes it to
Google Gemini (Google LLC) and returns what was read. Our server does not save the file or the result. Its logs
record only technical errors (an error message and code), never roster content. Google's handling is governed by the
[Gemini API terms]; we use a [paid] Gemini account, under which Google does not use the content to improve its
products. [Confirm the account type and log retention before launch.]

Before uploading, crop out anything you do not want to share, such as a staff number.

**Reminders**, the **home-screen widget** and the **lock-screen Live Activity** are made on your phone; they are not
sent through any server.

**Calendar (only if you switch it on):** the app writes your duties, getting ready, standby and planned sleep into a
separate "CrewCircadian" calendar on your phone. If your phone syncs its calendars to an account (for example iCloud
or Google), that calendar is synced like any other, under that account's terms. Switching the option off deletes it.

**App updates:** the app asks our server whether a newer version of its screens is available and downloads it. This
request contains no personal data; like any web request it reveals your IP address to the server, which does not
keep it beyond technical error logs.

**Feedback:** if you tap "Send feedback", a message opens in the app you choose (for example WhatsApp or Mail),
containing what you write plus the app version, phone model and screen. Nothing is sent unless you send it, and it
never includes your roster.

## What we never do

- Sell or rent data, or use it for advertising.
- Share anything with an airline, employer or regulator.
- Use roster data to train AI models.
- Collect location, contacts, health records or device identifiers.

## Your rights

You can see everything the app holds (Settings → download a backup), correct it (edit any day in Month), delete it
(Settings → Delete all my data), and withdraw consent to AI reading (Settings). Because the data is on your device, these
take effect immediately. For anything else, contact the grievance officer above. You may also complain to the Data
Protection Board of India.

## Children

CrewCircadian is for adults (18+) working as airline crew.

## Not medical advice

CrewCircadian gives general wellness guidance. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose or treat any condition,
and does not assess fitness for duty. If you feel fatigued, follow your airline's fatigue procedure, whatever the app
shows.
