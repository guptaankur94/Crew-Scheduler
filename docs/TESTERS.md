# Messages for testers

Ready to paste into WhatsApp. Replace the parts in [brackets].

## Invitation (before you have the links)

> Hi [name]! I'm building **CrewCircadian**, a calm planner for cabin crew: upload your roster screenshot and it
> works out when to sleep, nap, have caffeine and eat around your duties, with your flying hours for the month.
> No ads, and your roster stays on your phone.
>
> Would you test it for 2–3 weeks? It takes a few minutes to set up, and your feedback shapes the app. Testers get
> the paid features free for life when they come.
>
> Are you on iPhone or Android? For iPhone I need the email of your Apple ID; for Android, your Gmail.

## iPhone (TestFlight)

> Thanks for testing! On your iPhone:
> 1. Install **TestFlight** from the App Store (Apple's app for testing new apps).
> 2. Open this link on the iPhone: [TestFlight public link] → **Accept** → **Install**.
> 3. Open CrewCircadian → go to **Rosters** → upload a screenshot of your roster → check what was read → Save.
> 4. Optional: Settings → **Reminders and alarms** (wake-up alarm, leave-home reminder) and **Keep my phone calendar
>    updated**. Long-press your home screen → Edit → Add Widget → CrewCircadian for the next step at a glance.
>
> Something wrong or missing? Tap **Send feedback** at the bottom of any screen. The app updates itself in the
> background, so just keep using it.

## Android (Play closed test)

> Thanks for testing! On your Android phone:
> 1. Open this link and tap **Become a tester**: [Play testing link] (sign in with the Gmail you gave me).
> 2. Then open: [Play Store link] → **Install**.
> 3. Open CrewCircadian → **Rosters** → upload a screenshot of your roster → check what was read → Save.
> 4. Optional: Settings → **Reminders and alarms** and **Keep my phone calendar updated**. Long-press your home
>    screen → Widgets → CrewCircadian for the next step at a glance.
>
> Please keep the app installed for **at least 14 days**: Google requires 14 days of testing before the app can be
> published. Feedback: tap **Send feedback** at the bottom of any screen.
