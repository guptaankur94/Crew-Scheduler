# Competitor landscape: crew and traveller apps for sleep, circadian rhythm, fatigue and rosters (as of Oct 2026)

Notes on method: App Store ratings are from the US storefront, accessed 1 Oct 2026, unless stated otherwise. Google Play pages could not be fetched (they load with JavaScript), so Android ratings come only from secondary sources, which are flagged. Reddit could not be reached (the crawler is blocked), so crew sentiment comes from App Store reviews and trade or blog sources. Facts marked "(search excerpt)" came from search-result summaries and were not confirmed by fetching the full page.

## Q1. Consumer circadian, jet-lag and sleep-timing apps

### Takeaway
Timeshifter leads the consumer market. It has about 1.6 to 1.7M users, airline loyalty partnerships and new funding to expand into shift work, but it explicitly says it is "not intended for pilots and flight crew on duty" and has no roster import. Clairoe (formerly Arcashift) is the closest functional match to CrewCircadian, covering light, meals, caffeine, workouts and sleep, but it is generic and calendar-based. Wearables (Whoop, Oura, Ultrahuman, Garmin) are adding jet-lag advice. No consumer app found is built for cabin crew.

### Cited Findings

**Timeshifter (Timeshifter Inc.)**
- The jet-lag app has these features: personalised plans by chronotype, sleep pattern and itinerary; "Circadian Time", "Practicality Filter" and "Quick Turnaround" modes; light exposure and melatonin to shift the clock, plus low-dose caffeine and naps. The company claims followers were 16x less likely to report very severe jet lag (80,000+ post-flight surveys). — [Timeshifter jet lag app](https://www.timeshifter.com/jet-lag-app)
- Pricing: first plan free, a single plan costs $9.99, and an annual subscription with unlimited plans costs $24.99/year. - US App Store: 4.7★ (about 2,600 ratings). In-app purchases of $9.99 (plan), $24.99 (subscription) and $4.99 (discounted plan). Version 2.6.7 (22 Sept 2026). The listing states the app is "**not intended for pilots and flight crew on duty**." — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/timeshifter/id1380684374)
- The company claims 96.4% of users who followed its advice reported no severe jet lag (129,852 surveys) and 4.7★ from 6,000+ reviews. It calls AI-generated jet-lag advice "inconsistent and wrong" and says calculators produce "static schedules". — [Timeshifter comparison page](https://www.timeshifter.com/jet-lag/jet-lag-app-comparison)
- Shift Work app: advice on light, sleep windows, caffeine and melatonin, with fatigue-risk prediction and commute handling. ### Inferences
- Clairoe is the strongest feature-level threat because it already covers CrewCircadian's five pillars. Its small rating count (59) suggests limited reach.
- Timeshifter's crew disclaimer is probably a liability stance. It leaves room for a crew-specific product, but Timeshifter has the science brand, the funding and airline relationships to change that.
- Consumer sleep apps are losing subscribers (Sleep Cycle), which suggests generic sleep apps are saturated. A niche, roster-aware product is the more defensible position.

### Gaps
- Entrain's current availability is unverified.
- No Google Play download counts or ratings were collected for any app.
- Calm's sleep features were not researched.
- An unverified search claim that Rise was "one of Apple's Best Apps of 2026" was excluded.
- No independent trials of Timeshifter or Clairoe were found. The cited outcome figures are company surveys.

## Q2. Crew roster apps and airline-provided crew apps

### Takeaway
RosterBuster, owned by CAE since 2021, is the default third-party roster app: about 400k users, 500+ airlines, ad-supported freemium, and many reliability complaints. An Indian rival, AirRoster from Wingman Avtech, is free and ad-free with India and Gulf content. None of the roster apps, and none of the airline crew apps checked (AIMS eCrew/IndiGo iCrew, CAE Crew Access, Lufthansa CrewLink, Qatar's crew app), give individual crew guidance on sleep, light, meals or exercise.

### Gaps

## Q3. Enterprise fatigue tools, airline programmes and airline–consumer partnerships

### Takeaway
Enterprise biomathematical models (SAFTE-FAST, Boeing Alertness Model/CrewAlert, CAS, Pulsar, NetLine Fatigue) are built for airline planners and fatigue-risk management. The one crew-facing app among them, CrewAlert, is an older iOS pilot tool. The target airlines have no crew sleep apps: Air India has generic wellness programmes plus a strict BMI policy. Timeshifter's airline deals (United, Lufthansa Group, oneworld) are aimed at passengers and loyalty members, not crew. India's DGCA is tightening fatigue rules, which works in CrewCircadian's favour.

### Cited Findings
- **SAFTE-FAST** is a biomathematical fatigue model used by the FAA and the US Federal Railroad Administration (search excerpt). It is built into crew software such as Leon (Crew Timeline fatigue alerts). — [Leon](https://www.leonsoftware.com/blog/real-time-fatigue-analysis-with-safte-fast-in-leon.html)
- FAA field validation report on biomathematical fatigue models. — [FAA report](https://www.faa.gov/sites/faa.gov/files/data_research/research/med_humanfacs/oamtechreports/201212.pdf)
- **Jeppesen CrewAlert / CrewAlert Pro**: an iOS app built on the Boeing Alertness Model. It loads rosters automatically, reads sleep from Apple Health, predicts fatigue and suggests countermeasures. The Lite version is free; Pro costs about $30 once. — [Jeppesen](https://ww2.jeppesen.com/airline-crew-optimization-solutions/fatigue-risk-management/crewalert/); [App Store](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/crewalert-pro/id686458117)
- CrewAlert Pro launched in Sept 2013. — [Avstop](http://avstop.com/september_2013/jeppesen_introduces_app_to_manage_fatigue_risk_for_airline_crew_crewalert_pro.htm)
- Thoma Bravo completed its $10.55B purchase of Jeppesen, ForeFlight and related businesses from Boeing. Jeppesen ForeFlight now operates as a standalone company. — [GovConWire](https://www.govconwire.com/articles/thoma-bravo-boeing-digital-aviation-deal)
- Layoffs followed the sale. — [AVweb](https://avweb.com/aviation-news/jeppesen-foreflight-layoffs-following-pe/)
- **CAS-5 (CIRCADIAN)**: a cloud tool that gives a 0–100 fatigue-risk score. It covers pilots, flight attendants and ground crew, and needs at least 7 days of schedule history. — [CIRCADIAN](https://circadian.com/software/cas)
- **Pulsar Informatics**: the Aviation Fatigue Meter app includes a psychomotor vigilance test (PVT) for alertness, and the company's tech was originally developed for NASA, the US Department of Defense and the US Department of Transportation. — [Pulsar](https://pulsarinformatics.com/products/aviation)
- Air Canada selected Pulsar's fatigue risk management programme. — [Newswire](https://www.newswire.com/news/pulsar-informatics-comprehensive-fatigue-risk-management-program-21927376)
- **Air India**: from 1 May 2026, cabin crew fitness is assessed by BMI; crew with BMI ≥30 lose pay and are taken off flight rosters. The airline cites programmes "around nutrition, wellness, mental health, fatigue management and exercise". No app is mentioned. — [Fox News, 2 Apr 2026](https://www.foxnews.com/travel/airline-cracks-down-crews-weight-fitness-health-warning-some-could-pulled-from-flights)
- Headline: DGCA warned Air India over "systemic lapses in crew fatigue management training" (date not retrieved). — [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/india%2Fdgca-warns-air-india-over-systemic-lapses-in-crew-fatigue-management-training-3646114)
- **DGCA cabin crew draft rules (Oct 2025)**: weekly rest raised from 36 to 48 hours, tighter night-flying limits, and a separate hotel room for each crew member on layovers. The Federation of Indian Airlines (IndiGo, Air India, SpiceJet) lobbied to soften them. — [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/corporate/air-india-indigo-lobby-dgca-to-ease-draft-cabin-crew-fatigue-regulations); [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/indigo-air-india-lobby-government-to-soften-new-fatigue-rules-for-cabin-crew-3895934)
- **Pilot FDTL rules**: phased in from 1 July and 1 November. Weekly rest of 48 hours, "night" redefined as 00:00–06:00, and night landings capped at 2 (search excerpt). — [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/news/dgca-revises-duty-time-limitations-norms-for-flight-crew-raises-weekly-rest-periods-to-48-hrs)
- These rules contributed to IndiGo's December 2025 disruption. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_IndiGo_scheduling_crisis)
- **IndiGo cabin crew conditions** (unofficial): 8–15 days off per month; airport and home standby. Reported pay: freshers ₹40–55k a month, 2–5 years ₹60–85k; flying allowance about ₹1,000/hour; 60–90 flying hours a month. Domestic per diem ₹1,000–2,500; international $30–100. — [Wingman, 12 Aug 2026](https://www.wingmanlog.in/post/indigo-cabin-crew-roster-explained/)
- **Emirates**: long-haul crew rest in bunks in rotating "waves" planned around meal services, with about 3–4 hours lying down. — [Simple Flying](https://simpleflying.com/crew-rest-sleep-long-haul-emirates-airbus-a380-flights/)
- **Etihad**: the "Wellbeing" programme is for passengers, not crew. — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/uae/etihad-announces-wellness-programme-to-assure-passengers-while-flying-1.71799983)
- **Timeshifter airline partnerships**:
  - United (from 13 Nov 2019; extended 2024 and Nov 2025). — [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/united-airlines-puts-jet-lag-to-rest-with-timeshifter-app-partnership-300957683.html); [Timeshifter news](https://www.timeshifter.com/news)
  - Lufthansa Group (Oct 2024): Miles & More members earn miles; crew are not mentioned. — [Timeshifter](https://www.timeshifter.com/news/timeshifter-hits-1-million-users-for-its-jet-lag-app-and-forms-new-partnership-with-lufthansa-group)
  - oneworld (8 Sept 2026): free subscriptions with round-the-world tickets. — [Business Traveller](https://www.businesstraveller.com/news/oneworld-timeshifter-free-jet-lag-plans-rtw-tickets/)
  - Other partners: IHG, SAP Concur, and SAP (Shift Work app integration, 2021). — [Timeshifter news](https://www.timeshifter.com/news)
- **Cabin crew sleep evidence** (search excerpt of a scoping review): sleep is most disturbed the night before duty, on layovers, and on the first night home. Domestic fatigue is linked to duty hours, number of landings and layover length; international fatigue to flight length and time zones. — [PMC scoping review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9916232/)

### Inferences
- Airlines buy fatigue models to plan safe rosters, not to coach individual crew. Individual countermeasures (when to nap, eat, take caffeine, seek light) are left to crew, and that is CrewCircadian's space.
- The DGCA rule changes and Air India's fitness and BMI policy raise Indian crew demand for fatigue, meal and exercise planning. An airline could also commission such a tool itself, which is a threat.

### Gaps
- No confirmed crew-wellbeing apps were found at IndiGo, Emirates, Etihad or Qatar.
- The source for "Emirates predictive fatigue modelling" could not be identified.
- The final status of the cabin crew draft rules in 2026 is unconfirmed.
- An "84% of crew fatigued" figure surfaced without a clear source and was excluded.

## Q4. Product-by-product comparison

### Takeaway

## Q5. White space and threats

### Takeaway
The white space is a quiet, ad-free, roster-aware daily planner for cabin crew. It would turn Indian and Gulf rosters (including multi-sector domestic days, red-eyes and standby) into timed advice on sleep, naps, light, caffeine, meals and workouts, plus salary estimates. No product found does this. The main threats are Timeshifter extending into crew, Clairoe adding roster import, CAE/RosterBuster or AirRoster adding wellness features, and airlines or wearables providing tools.

### Cited Findings
- Timeshifter excludes "pilots and flight crew on duty" ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/timeshifter/id1380684374)). Yet it has new funding to expand into shift work ([release](https://www.timeshifter.com/news/timeshifter-raises-new-funding-to-scale-circadian-technology-across-travel-shift-work-and-healthcare)) and partnerships with airlines and oneworld ([news](https://www.timeshifter.com/news)).
- Clairoe already times light, meals, caffeine, workouts and sleep, with shift-work and jet-lag modes. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/arcashift-sleep-balance/id1589005077)
- RosterBuster already holds roster data for about 400k crew and is owned by CAE, which also sells crew management systems to airlines. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/rosterbuster-crew-app/id1035558169); [AIN](https://www.ainonline.com/aviation-news/business-aviation/2021-04-06/cae-acquires-rosterbuster-crew-management-app)
- AirRoster is free and ad-free, targets India and the Gulf, and has no fatigue or pay features yet. — [AirRoster](https://www.wingmanlog.in/product-airroster-airline-roster-app-pilots-cabin-crew/)
- Wearables are adding jet-lag advice: Whoop in 2026, plus Garmin and Ultrahuman. — [Gadgets & Wearables](https://gadgetsandwearables.com/2026/04/06/whoop-jet-lag-advisor/)
- Experts stress that light exposure is the core lever and that evidence for apps is limited. — [Global News](https://globalnews.ca/news/10550125/jet-lag-apps-travel/)
- Timeshifter attacks AI-generated advice as unreliable. — [Timeshifter](https://www.timeshifter.com/jet-lag/jet-lag-app-comparison)
- Regulatory tailwind: DGCA's cabin crew fatigue drafts ([Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/corporate/air-india-indigo-lobby-dgca-to-ease-draft-cabin-crew-fatigue-regulations)) and Air India's fitness and BMI policy ([Fox News](https://www.foxnews.com/travel/airline-cracks-down-crews-weight-fitness-health-warning-some-could-pulled-from-flights)).
- IndiGo crew roster pain: frequent changes, standby, session timeouts and manual tracking of hours. — [Wingman](https://www.wingmanlog.in/post/indigo-cabin-crew-roster-explained/)

### Inferences
- **Differentiators**:
  - Cabin-crew-specific logic: early reports, multi-sector days, standby, layovers, short Gulf turnarounds.
  - Meals and workouts that fit duty patterns.
  - Indian and Gulf salary estimates; no competitor found does this.
  - No ads.
  - Clear "why" explanations grounded in light-first science.
  - AI used only to read rosters, with the user confirming the parsed result, so advice is never AI-generated. This answers the AI-reliability critique.
- **Trust risks**: a misread roster produces wrong advice. Melatonin and medication advice may conflict with crew medical rules (not verified). Fatigue guidance should be framed as lifestyle advice and should not imply fitness-for-duty decisions, similar to Timeshifter's disclaimer.
- **Highest threats**:
  1. Timeshifter launching a crew mode with roster import.
  2. RosterBuster (CAE) or AirRoster adding sleep plans on top of the rosters they already hold.
  3. Airlines (Air India, Emirates) commissioning their own crew wellness tools.
  4. Ultrahuman, Whoop or Oura bundling free jet-lag guidance.

### Gaps
- No direct crew sentiment was gathered (Reddit was blocked).
- It is unknown whether Timeshifter, CAE or Wingman plan crew-wellness features.
- Willingness to pay among Indian crew is untested.
